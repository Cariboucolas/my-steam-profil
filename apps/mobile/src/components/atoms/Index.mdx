# Atoms

The smallest pieces the screens are built from: a chip, a ring, a bar, a skeleton. An atom knows
nothing about Steam — it draws a number, a label or a shape it is handed, and nothing more.

## What an atom may do

- Take primitives through its props: a percentage, a label, a size.
- Hold the local state of a control, and animate within what the reader's motion setting allows.

## What it may not do

- Take a domain object. A `GameDto` or a `LibrarySummary` belongs one level up.
- Fetch anything. Only a page loads what it shows ([ADR-0022](https://github.com/Cariboucolas/my-steam-profil/blob/main/docs/adr/0022-only-a-page-loads-what-it-shows.md)).

## Its stories

Inline args, and the values the app itself passes where it passes a fixed one — the ring the
library card draws is 72 across, so its story is too. A value the app never has is not a state
worth showing.

## What a story here is trusted for

The state, never the pixels. The gallery runs on the web, and the device settles how anything
looks ([ADR-0021](https://github.com/Cariboucolas/my-steam-profil/blob/main/docs/adr/0021-a-story-shows-a-state-the-device-settles-how-it-looks.md)).
